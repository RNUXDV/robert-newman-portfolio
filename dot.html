DOT

A next-gen social experience designed to replace mindless scrolling with intentional, orbit-based exploration — prioritizing user autonomy, emotional safety, and meaningful connection.

Back to Home Jump to Overview

Overview

My role: Product vision, UX strategy, interaction design, prototyping, early implementation.

Outcome: A coherent concept + working HTML/CSS prototype foundation to evolve into a product demo.

Timeline: Ongoing

The Problem

Traditional social platforms optimize for engagement metrics, which often leads to compulsive scrolling, distorted incentives, and emotionally draining experiences. People want connection — but not at the cost of their attention, mental health, or sense of agency.

The Insight

Healthy social behavior is more like a personal “universe” than a feed: centered on the individual, with relationships and interests that deepen over time. DOT reframes discovery as intentional exploration rather than infinite consumption.

The Solution

Core mechanics

  • Center Dot: the user as the anchor of their experience
  • Orbit Dots: interests and spaces curated by the user
  • Radial navigation: an alternative to infinite scrolling
  • Inner circle: a small trusted network for sharing without validation pressure
  • Soft fade: relationships drift naturally instead of hard “unfollow” dynamics
